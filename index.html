Is the numerology reading free?

Name and date-of-birth numbers are free. Some detailed sections may be marked premium by the astrologer.

What does 13 / 4 mean?

13 is your compound number (the letters added up). 4 is the root, found by adding 1 + 3. Both are shown because each can carry its own meaning.

What if there is no interpretation for my number?

You will see a clear note saying none is configured yet. We never make up a prediction.

Is my data private?

Your details are stored in our private database and are used only to prepare your readings.
